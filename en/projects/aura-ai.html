Aura.AI

A WhatsApp assistant for boutique jewellery stores that handles orders, stock, shipping and customer messages in one place.

Context

Highlights

  1. A Gemini 2.5 Flash agent with function calling (13 tools) answers customers on WhatsApp: order status, stock and shipping
  2. RAG search over the product catalogue with Gemini embeddings
  3. Inventory, orders, suppliers and shipment tracking in a FastAPI + SQLite backend, managed from a web admin panel
