DocMind-AI

Upload PDF, Word or text files and ask questions about them; the answers come only from your documents.

Highlights

  2. Llama 3.1 8B on Groq generates answers within seconds
  3. Streamlit interface, plus a FastAPI service with streaming responses
  4. Dockerised for a one-command setup
